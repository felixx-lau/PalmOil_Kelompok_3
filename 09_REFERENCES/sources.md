## Palm Oil Agriculture and Processing Process Mining — 24SI1 Group 03

### 1. Sumber Data Keuangan & Nilai Tukar Resmi
1. **Bank Indonesia (BI)**:
   - *Statistik Informasi Kurs Transaksi Bank Indonesia*. Diakses untuk data transaksi tanggal 13 Oktober 2026.
   - URL: https://www.bi.go.id/id/statistik/informasi-kurs/transaksi-bi/default.aspx

### 2. Standar Agronomi & Pengolahan Kelapa Sawit (Palm Oil Industry Literature)
2. **Malaysian Palm Oil Board (MPOB)**:
   - *MPOB Oil Palm Fruit Grading Manual (3rd Edition)*. Standard specifications for fresh fruit bunches (FFB), ripeness fractions (unripe, underripe, ripe, overripe, rotten), and foreign matter thresholds in commercial mills.
   - *Palm Oil Factory Process Operations & Engineering Handbook*. Principles of fruit sterilization steam pressure (2.8–3.0 bar), fruit threshing efficiency, digester mechanical heating, screw pressing, and clarification centrifuge operations.
3. **Roundtable on Sustainable Palm Oil (RSPO)**:
   - *RSPO Principles and Criteria for the Production of Sustainable Palm Oil*. Traceability protocols from plantation estate blocks to mill weighbridges and storage tanks.
4. **Sivasothy, K. et al. (2005)**:
   - *Continuous Sterilization of Oil Palm Fruit Bunches*. Journal of Oil Palm Research, Vol. 17, pp. 106–118. Technical baseline on steam cycle optimization and reduction of sterilization bottlenecks.
5. **Chow, M. C. & Ho, C. C. (2002)**:
   - *Chemical and Physical Characteristics of Crude Palm Oil*. Mesocarp lipase activation post-harvest, enzymatic lipolysis kinetics, and Free Fatty Acid (FFA) build-up during ambient transport delays.

### 3. Metodologi Process Mining & Rekayasa Data
6. **van der Aalst, Wil M. P. (2016)**:
   - *Process Mining: Data Science in Action (2nd Edition)*. Springer Berlin Heidelberg. Foundations of event log preparation, Alpha and inductive process discovery, conformance checking, and bottleneck queuing analysis.
7. **IEEE Task Force on Process Mining (2012)**:
   - *Process Mining Manifesto*. Standards on event log maturity levels, data cleaning integrity, and distinguishing authentic process variants from duplicate transactional noise.
8. **Celonis Academic Alliance**:
   - *Process Mining Analysis & Execution Management Guide*. Celonis Studio visual exploration, variant explorer, and throughput time calculation methodology.
9. **DuckDB Foundation**:
   - *DuckDB Official Documentation (v1.1+)*. High-performance analytical in-process SQL execution, multi-format timestamp parsing, safe typing with TRY_CAST, and window functions for transactional deduplication.
